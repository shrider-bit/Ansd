How to play

Survivor

Find the key, unlock the front door and escape before time runs out. Hide in wardrobes, the pantry or under the bed. Running is loud and tires you out!

Granny

Listen for footsteps and doors, call the survivor's name with your voice buttons, search hiding spots and catch them before they escape.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · C crouch · E use / hide · F flashlight · 1-5 Granny voices · Esc pause

Phone / tablet

Left thumb: move · Right thumb: look · USE, RUN, DUCK, LIGHT buttons. Turn your phone sideways.

Two players

One player taps CREATE ROOM and reads out the code. The other taps JOIN ROOM. After each round you swap roles automatically.
